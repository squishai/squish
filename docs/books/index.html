About Konjo Publishing

Where Squish Came From

Wesley Scholl

Author, Learning with Squish

Squish wasn't supposed to be a children's book character. He started as a mascot for a completely different project — until my daughter saw an early version of him on my screen and loved him immediately.

So I kept refining the character, and started making up stories about him and her, playing together. She asked for them again and again — "I want Squish book" — until one Father's Day, I turned one into a real book for her shelf. She still pulls it down and hands it to me herself.

That's still what these books are: a character who comes back, and a story worth asking for twice.

Questions Parents Actually Ask

What age is this for?

Ages 1–5 across the series. Learning Shapes with Squish leans a little older, but every book is built for read-aloud time starting in toddlerhood.

How long is each book?

Short on purpose — from 10 rhyming spreads (Habits) to 20 rhyming pages (Counting). Built for a wiggly toddler's attention span, not a chapter-book sitting.

Do I need to read them in order?

Nope — every book stands alone. If you want the official order, it's Habits → Counting → Colors → the Collection bind-up → Shapes, but Squish and Lilyan will happily meet your kid wherever you start.

Is there anything in it for the grown-up reading it?

Yes — every book ends with a real "Note for Grown-Ups," with simple tips for reading it together, not just a cute story for your kid.
