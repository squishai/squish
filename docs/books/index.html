About the Author

Wesley SchollAuthor, Learning with Squish

Wesley Scholl is the author behind Learning with Squish, a board book series he created for his daughter, Lily. It started as a way to make everyday routines — brushing teeth, counting toys, naming colors — feel like play instead of a power struggle.

That idea grew into Konjo Publishing's Learning with Squish books: sweet, simple stories starring a squishy purple friend named Squish, made for tiny hands and short attention spans, and built to be read together, again and again, until every page is memorized by heart.
